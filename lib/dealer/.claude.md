## Principal and host
- **`DEALER` is not a `UserRole`.** `hasMinRole`/`canDo`/`requireApiRole` refuse it, but
  dozens of call sites treat any non-CLIENT session as staff. The boundary is the host
  gate, `lib/proxy/dealer-host.ts`: on `DEALER_APP_HOST` only `/dealer`, `/api/dealer`,
  NextAuth and the set-password token path are served and a non-DEALER session 404s; off
  it the dealer paths 404 and a DEALER session is refused. A new staff guard joins the
  census, `lib/dealer/staff-guards-refuse.test.ts`.
  Unset `DEALER_APP_HOST` = no dealer sign-in anywhere; it needs a `TENANT_HOST_MAP`
  entry and runs on the staff container (`APP_MODE=client` refuses it).
- `sign-in.ts`; revalidation `principal.ts` (the jwt DEALER branch runs before staff).
  Revocation is `tokenVersion` (DB: only increases): deactivating a user or dealer bumps
  it in the same transaction. Portal on = `featureAssetFinance && featureDealerPortal &&
  isDemoLender` (CHECK `TenantConfig_dealer_portal_demo_only` until dealer 2FA ships).
  Dealer-host writes must be same-origin; `withDealer` repeats the proxy check.

## The 404 rule
- Every refusal of a signed-in session is 404 (`guards.ts`, `page-guard.ts`): another
  dealer's or lender's row, a staff/client session, the portal off, SALES on an admin
  route. Every query filters `{ tenantId, dealerId }` (`dealerDealWhere` in `deals.ts`);
  a miss is `dealerMiss` (`result.ts`). Never 403 or "not yours".
- A duplicate borrower or asset is 409 "already on the lender's book", showing nothing of
  the existing row. A dealer deal names only a borrower and asset that dealer captured;
  `createdByDealerId` and a dealer deal's parties are write-once (DB).

## Projection and handoff
- `deal-view.ts` is a whitelist `select`, never the deal row. Never add: decision or
  reasons, approval exceptions, financials, affordability, bureau/screening, NCA
  regime/rule/override, payout, staff uploads, other dealers' rows, or how the loan
  performs (a commission reversal's reason). `deal-view.test.ts` plants sentinels;
  extend it with any new field. The commission statement follows the same rule.
- Dealer submit only sets `dealerSubmittedAt` (DRAFT is left only after it, CHECK
  `AssetFinanceDeal_dealer_handover`); the staff accept (`submit.ts`) runs the unchanged
  `submitDeal` as staff. Consent is evidence, only for people the dealer captured; staff
  verify writes `ClientConsent` (`consent.ts`). Dealer uploads never satisfy staff gates;
  after the hand-over they only answer an open request.
- Dealer-typed contact details are untrusted until staff verify them
  (`contact-verification.ts`): no portal activation or password (DB), no signing code.

## Commission (the database owns it)
- No TS writer. `guardAssetDealDealer` snapshots on the move into APPROVED (callers
  refused; base = goods advance: cash price incl. VAT less deposit and trade-in);
  `accrueDealerCommission` writes EARNED at BOOKED; `clawbackDealerCommission` REVERSED
  on the Loan's status (CANCELLED always, else inside `clawbackMonths`). Insert-only.
- **Commission must never depend on the rate or the fees**: a dealer paid more for
  pricing the borrower up has a conflict of interest against the borrower.
- Never a borrower charge: the cost-of-credit graph must not import `lib/dealer/*` or read
  `dealerCommission*` (`no-borrower-charge.test.ts`).
- **Netting.** A run links REVERSED lines first; an EARNED line joins only with or after
  its reversal, and waits if that falls after `periodEnd` (`payable-run-lines.ts`;
  triggers `DealerPayableRunLine_netting`, `DealerPayableRun_netting`). Four-eyes CHECK,
  release only to an APPROVED bank account (staff-captured), one live run per line.
  Journals and the payable control: `lib/accounting/.claude.md`.
