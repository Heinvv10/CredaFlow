## Principal and host
- **`DEALER` is not a `UserRole`.** `hasMinRole`/`canDo`/`requireApiRole` refuse it, but many
  call sites treat any non-CLIENT session as staff. The boundary is the host gate,
  `lib/proxy/dealer-host.ts`: on `DEALER_APP_HOST` only dealer paths, NextAuth and set-password
  are served (a non-DEALER session 404s); off it dealer paths 404 and a DEALER session is
  refused. New staff guards join `lib/dealer/staff-guards-refuse.test.ts`. Unset host = no
  dealer sign-in; it needs a `TENANT_HOST_MAP` entry and the staff container.
- `sign-in.ts`; revalidation `principal.ts` (the jwt DEALER branch runs before staff).
  Revocation is `tokenVersion` (DB: only increases), bumped with any deactivation. Portal
  on = `featureAssetFinance && featureDealerPortal && isDemoLender` (CHECK
  `TenantConfig_dealer_portal_demo_only` until dealer 2FA ships). Writes are same-origin.

## The 404 rule
- Every refusal of a signed-in session is 404 (`guards.ts`, `page-guard.ts`): another dealer's
  or lender's row, a staff/client session, the portal off, SALES on an admin route. Every
  query filters `{ tenantId, dealerId }` (`dealerDealWhere`); a miss is `dealerMiss`. Never 403.
- A duplicate borrower or asset is 409 "already on the lender's book" (audited, rate
  limited), showing nothing of it. A dealer deal names only a borrower and asset that
  dealer captured; `createdByDealerId` and a dealer deal's parties are write-once (DB).

## Projection and handoff
- `deal-view.ts` is a whitelist `select`. Never add: decision or reasons, approval exceptions,
  financials, affordability, bureau/screening, NCA regime/override, payout, staff uploads, other
  dealers' rows, how the loan performs. `deal-view.test.ts` plants sentinels.
- Dealer submit only sets `dealerSubmittedAt` (DRAFT is left only after it, CHECK
  `AssetFinanceDeal_dealer_handover`); the staff accept (`submit.ts`) runs the unchanged
  `submitDeal` as staff. Consent is evidence, only for people the dealer captured, v2 with
  how it was signed (REMOTE needs the identity check; v1 rows frozen). Dealer uploads never
  satisfy staff gates; after the hand-over they only answer a request.
- Cancel (`cancel.ts`): own DRAFT before hand-over, ADMIN or its creator (DB
  `guardAssetDealDealerCancel`). Purge (`draft-purge.ts`): the DB names the deal
  (`dealerDealPurgeReason`: 90 days unsent, 3 years once handed over, never a loan) and
  erases a party itself on `dealerPurgedAt`; anything linked elsewhere is kept.
- Dealer-typed contact details are untrusted until staff verify them
  (`contact-verification.ts`): no portal activation or password, no signing code, no
  booking (DB: `Client_dealer_contact_guard`, `AssetFinanceDeal_dealer_contacts_verified`).

## Commission (the database owns it)
- No TS writer. `guardAssetDealDealer` snapshots on the move into APPROVED (base = goods
  advance: cash price incl. VAT less deposit and trade-in); EARNED at BOOKED, REVERSED
  on the Loan's status (CANCELLED always, else inside `clawbackMonths`). Insert-only.
- **Commission never depends on the rate or fees**, never a borrower charge (`no-borrower-charge.test.ts`).
- **Netting.** A run links REVERSED lines first; an EARNED line joins only with or after
  its reversal (`payable-run-lines.ts`; triggers `DealerPayableRunLine_netting`,
  `DealerPayableRun_netting`). Four-eyes; release to the APPROVED staff-captured account,
  with the dealer's tax invoice if it carries VAT. Journals: `lib/accounting/.claude.md`.
