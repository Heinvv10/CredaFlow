5.1 Your cancellation
You gave us notice on 23 February 2027 to cancel your lease, as this agreement allows on 20 business days' notice. Section
14(2)(b)(i)(bb) of the CPA does not apply to a juristic lessee, but this agreement gives you the same terms as the CPA gives a
natural-person consumer. Your lease therefore ends on 24 March 2027, whether or not you accept or pay this quote. The 14
rentals that would fall due after that date are cancelled. You return the goods on that date; they are inspected, and any
damage beyond fair wear and tear, any usage above the contracted usage and, on a full-maintenance lease, the settlement of
the maintenance fund are invoiced separately on the end-of-term tax invoice. They are not part of this quote.
