7.4 Interest and changes to the contract rate
Interest accrues daily on the outstanding capital at the contract rate ("the Contract Rate"), calculated on the actual number of
days elapsed in a year of 365 days, and is payable as part of each payment in the Schedule.
The Contract Rate is linked to a reference rate: it is ZARONIA plus 1.00% a year. On the schedule start date the reference
rate is 10.75% (effective 29 May 2026), so the Contract Rate is 11.75% a year (nominal). "ZARONIA" means the South
African Rand Overnight Index Average published by the South African Reserve Bank; each published change applies from its
benchmark effective date.
