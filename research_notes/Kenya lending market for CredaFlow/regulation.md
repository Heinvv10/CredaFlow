# Kenyan lending regulation (as of November 2026): what a lender must do, and how it differs from SA/NCA

Research date: 2026-11-06. Primary texts read directly: CBK (Digital Credit Providers) Regulations 2022 (LN 46/2022, full PDF) and Banking (Credit Reference Bureau) Regulations 2020 (LN 55/2020, full PDF). The new **CBK (Non-Deposit Taking Credit Providers) Regulations 2026 (LN 191/2026)** could NOT be read in full (Kenya Law fetch failed); everything about them below comes from law-firm/press summaries and should be checked against the gazetted text.

## 1. Licensing tiers and 2024–2026 changes

### Takeaway
The big 2026 change: since **29 Sept 2026** every non-deposit-taking credit business, digital or not, falls under CBK under the NDTCP Regulations 2026 (LN 191). These replace the 2022 DCP Regulations. Lenders with KES 20m+ capital or loan book need a licence. Smaller lenders need registration. Existing unlicensed lenders must apply by **29 March 2027**. Banks, MFBs and SACCOs stay under their own Acts. Hire purchase is excluded.

### Cited Findings
- The Business Laws (Amendment) Act No. 20 of 2024 was assented 11 Dec 2024 and commenced 27 Dec 2024. It amended the CBK Act so nobody may carry on "non-deposit taking credit business" unless CBK-licensed or permitted under another written law. It replaced "digital credit business" with a channel-neutral concept that covers asset finance and BNPL but excludes hire purchase agreements. — [Afriwise/CDH summary via search; CDH alert](https://www.cliffedekkerhofmeyr.com/en/news/publications/2025/Practice/Corporate-Commercial/corporate-commercial-alert-kenya-22-January-banking-and-finance-amendments-introduced-by-the-business-laws-amendment-act-20-of-2024); [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/kenya-enacts-business-laws-amendment-act-2024)
- The same Act amended the Microfinance Act so existing non-deposit-taking microfinance businesses must obtain a CBK licence within six months of commencement. It also introduced consumer-protection duties, including a ban on harassing borrowers. — [CDH](https://www.cliffedekkerhofmeyr.com/en/news/publications/2025/Practice/Corporate-Commercial/corporate-commercial-alert-kenya-22-January-banking-and-finance-amendments-introduced-by-the-business-laws-amendment-act-20-of-2024); [The Star, 6 Jan 2025](https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2025-01-06-its-now-illegal-to-harass-borrowers-as-new-law-takes-effect)
- Unlicensed operation reportedly carries up to 3 years' imprisonment or a KES 5m fine (s.33S CBK Act as amended). This comes from an informal guide and is not verified. — [paybillke guide](https://paybillke.com/guides/what-the-2024-law-changed-for-asset-financing-kenya)
- Draft NDTCP Regulations were published for comment on 7 Aug 2025. — [CBK press release](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/uploads/press_releases/166389007_Invitation%20for%20Comments%20on%20the%20Draft%20Central%20Bank%20of%20Kenya%20(Non-Deposit%20Taking%20Credit%20Providers)%20Regulations,%202025.pdf); [draft text](https://centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Draft-Central-Bank-of-Kenya-Non-Deposit-Taking-Credit-Providers-Regulations-2025.pdf)
- **Final regulations:** CBK (Non-Deposit Taking Credit Providers) Regulations 2026, Legal Notice 191 of 2026, dated 29 Sept 2026 and published in the Kenya Gazette. They replace the DCP Regulations 2022. — [Kenya Law LN 191/2026](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2026/191/eng@2026-09-29); [MMS Advocates](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/)
  - Conflict: an ITEdgeNews piece dated 1 Oct 2026 still described the regulations as "14 months in draft". It was probably written before gazettement was known, and Kenya Law plus several law firms confirm LN 191. — [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/)
- **Scope:** non-deposit-taking credit businesses not regulated under another law. Excluded are banks, MFIs, licensed SACCOs, co-operatives, credit guarantee companies, hire-purchase and lease-finance businesses regulated under their own laws, and credit merely incidental to the sale of goods or services. — [MMS Advocates](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/)
- **Licence vs registration (regs 5, 6, 9):**
  - Initial capital of KES 20m or more requires a licence. Below that, registration is required.
  - A registered provider must upgrade once its capital, borrowings or loan book exceeds KES 20m.
  - Every provider must be a Companies Act company with a physical registered office in Kenya, and significant shareholders, directors, the CEO and senior officers must be certified fit and proper.
  - [MMS Advocates](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/); the same threshold appeared in the draft per [Business Daily, 7 Aug 2025](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/cbk-sets-threshold-for-micro-lender-licences-5146584)
- **Fees:**
  - Application: KES 100,000 (previously KES 5,000).
  - Annual fee: KES 500,000 if licensed (previously KES 20,000), KES 250,000 if registered.
  - Paying the annual fee after 31 Dec adds a KES 1,000,000 charge.
  - [TechTrendsKE, 5 Oct 2026](https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/10/05/cbk-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations/)
- **Transition:** existing CBK-licensed DCPs are deemed licensed but must comply with the new operating requirements. Other existing providers must apply within six months, by 29 March 2027. — [MMS Advocates](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/)
- **Penalties (reg 86):**
  - Institutions: up to KES 2m, or 3x the gross gain made or loss avoided, whichever is higher, plus daily penalties.
  - Senior officers: up to KES 250,000 personally.
  - Other sanctions: lending restrictions, disqualification for up to 3 years, and suspension or revocation.
  - [MMS Advocates](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/)
- **Number of licensed DCPs:**
  - Jan 2026: ~195. [pesamarket](https://pesamarket.com/en/blog/cbk-licensed-digital-lenders-kenya-2026)
  - Apr 2026: 227 after 32 new licences. [HapaKenya, 15 Apr 2026](https://hapakenya.com/2026/04/15/cbk-licenses-32-more-digital-loan-companies/)
  - Jul 2026: 252. [CBK DCP directory, July 2026](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Directory-of-Digital-Credit-Providers-July-2026.pdf); [Dawan](https://www.dawan.africa/news/cbk-licenses-25-more-digital-lenders-bringing-total-to-252)
  - 30 Sep 2026: **281** after 29 more. [business.co.ke](https://business.co.ke/cbk-licenses-29-more-digital-credit-providers-raising-total-to-281/); [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/). The CBK press release itself was not found.
- **Backlog:** at least 574 digital-lender applications were pending in Aug 2025, and about 126 DCPs had CBK approval at the time. The 126 figure looks low against the later counts and may be a reporting error. — [Business Daily, 7 Aug 2025](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/cbk-sets-threshold-for-micro-lender-licences-5146584)
- **Book size:** by Aug 2026 licensed DCPs had disbursed 9,596,509 loans worth KES 165.1bn, an average of ~KES 17,200. — [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/)
- **SACCOs (SASRA):**
  - SASRA's 2024 report counted 177 deposit-taking (DT) SACCOs and 178 non-withdrawable deposit-taking (NWDT) SACCOs.
  - SASRA's mandate was extended to NWDT SACCOs with KES 100m+ in deposits from Jan 2021 (Sacco Societies (Non-Deposit Taking Business) Regulations 2020).
  - 176 SACCOs are licensed for deposit-taking for 2026, and 5 were restricted to credit-only.
  - A Sacco Societies (Amendment) Bill 2025 (tiered licensing, mandatory credit-information sharing) was still before Parliament in Apr 2026. Its status is unconfirmed.
  - [Search summary of SASRA/news sources incl. The Star, 9 Apr 2026](https://www.the-star.co.ke/business/markets/2026-04-09-tighter-rules-safety-nets-in-bold-shake-up-of-sacco-industry); [Kenya Law Sacco Societies Act consolidated](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2008/14/eng@2025-06-20)
- **Courts and unlicensed lenders:** a Nairobi Small Claims Court ruling in July 2026 held that unlicensed digital lenders cannot use the courts to recover loans. — [WeeTracker, 27 Jul 2026](https://weetracker.com/2026/07/27/kenyan-court-delivers-blow-to-unlicensed-loan-apps-seeking-debt-repayment/)

### Inferences
- A CredaFlow tenant in Kenya would mostly be an NDTCP: an asset or logbook lender, payroll lender, SME lender, or app lender. Banks, MFBs and SACCOs are a separate, heavier regime (prudential returns, deposit handling) that CredaFlow is not built for.
- The licensed/registered split maps to a tenant config flag. Both tiers appear to share the same conduct rules; only the fees and prudential intensity differ (unverified).
- SA contrast: the NCA requires NCR registration for credit providers with 100+ agreements or a R500k+ book. Kenya's line is a KES 20m capital/book threshold between licence and registration, but registration is still compulsory below it. Kenya carves hire purchase out (it has its own Act), whereas SA brings instalment agreements fully under the NCA.

### Gaps
- Full text of LN 191/2026 was not read. Still unconfirmed: regulation numbers, minimum capital for registrants, the exact complaints timeline, the collections conduct list in the new regs, and the outsourcing/cloud rules.
- Current status of the Microfinance Act six-month deadline, and whether credit-only MFIs moved under NDTCP or the Microfinance Act.
- Whether chamas and informal shylocks fall within "members of the public": MMS notes the term is undefined.
- Status of the Sacco Societies (Amendment) Bill 2025.

## 2. Interest rate and pricing rules

### Takeaway
There is no interest rate cap: the Banking Act s.33B cap was repealed in 2019, and NDTCPs have no ceiling and no minimum term. Control comes through **CBK prior written approval of every product and pricing change**, 30-day customer notice, and a statutory in-duplum-style recovery cap in the NDTCP and DCP regulations. Banks price variable loans on **KESONIA + K**.

### Cited Findings
- **Product and pricing approval:**
  - Under the 2022 DCP Regs, a DCP may not introduce a product or vary its features without CBK's prior written approval (reg 17(1)), nor change its pricing model or parameters without that approval (reg 29(1)). — [DCP Regulations 2022, LN 46](https://centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/L-.N.-No.-46-Central-Bank-of-Kenya-Digital-Credit-Providers-Regulations-2022.pdf)
  - Customers must get at least 30 days' notice of product variations (reg 17(2)). An increase in charges or credit limits, or any change to terms 27(1)(a)–(e), needs 30 days' notice **and customer acceptance** (reg 29(2)). — [same](https://centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/L-.N.-No.-46-Central-Bank-of-Kenya-Digital-Credit-Providers-Regulations-2022.pdf)
  - The NDTCP Regs 2026 keep this: prior written CBK approval for new products and for changes to features, interest rates or pricing parameters, plus 30 days' notice and customer acceptance. — [MMS](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/); [TechTrendsKE](https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/10/05/cbk-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations/)
- **No price ceiling:** CBK rules set no minimum loan term and no price ceiling. Disclosed APRs range from 30% to 4,432% (Koro max), 425–1,600% (Kashbean), and up to 270.24% (Mogo logbook loans). — [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/)
- **Statutory recovery cap (DCP Regs 2022 reg 19):** once a loan is non-performing, recovery is limited to:
  - the principal owing when the loan became non-performing;
  - contractual interest not exceeding that principal; and
  - reasonable recovery expenses.
  If the loan cures and later defaults again, the cap resets to the last time it became non-performing. Interest under a court order after judgment is not capped. — [DCP Regulations 2022](https://centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/L-.N.-No.-46-Central-Bank-of-Kenya-Digital-Credit-Providers-Regulations-2022.pdf)
  - The NDTCP Regs 2026 keep the same cap. They define NPL as unpaid for 30 days on loans of 30 days or less, or 90 days on longer loans, subject to the contract's default period. — [MMS](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/)
- **Repayment allocation order (NDTCP 2026):** principal first, then fees and charges, then interest. — [MMS](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/)
- **Foreign currency:** loans in foreign currencies are prohibited for NDTCPs. — [MMS](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/). The Competition Authority (CAK) fined Mogo KES 10,851,473.20 on 4 Oct 2024 over dollar-denominated loans and changed interest bases. — [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/)
- **Banking Act s.44A in duplum:** on its face it applies to banks and institutions licensed under the Banking Act. Case law on non-banks is split:
  - *Momentum Credit v Kabuiya* (2022) said it does not apply to a non-deposit-taker.
  - *Mugure v HELB* (2022) said it applies to all lenders, including digital lenders and MFIs.
  - A 2026 High Court Faulu Microfinance case applied it to MFIs.
  - On 2 Jun 2026 the Thika Small Claims Court cut Mogo's KES 677,381 claim on a KES 400,000 loan to KES 100,631.
  - A parliamentary committee recommended entrenching in duplum in the Consumer Protection Act. This is a recommendation, not enacted.
  - [Search synthesis of TripleOKLaw](https://www.tripleoklaw.com/capping-the-cost-of-credit-in-duplum-rule/); [thelawyer.africa](https://thelawyer.africa/2025/06/03/the-in-duplum-rule-beyond-banks/); [Pulse 2026](https://www.pulse.co.ke/story/court-ruling-confirms-reach-of-the-in-duplum-rule-in-debt-recovery-by-lenders-2026042207372395355); [Eastleigh Voice](https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/headlines/387341/parliament-backs-plan-to-cap-interest-charged-by-digital-lenders-saccos); [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/)
- **Revised risk-based credit pricing model (banks):**
  - Total lending rate = KESONIA + premium K. Total cost of credit = KESONIA + K + fees and charges.
  - It applies to variable-rate loans, excluding FX and fixed-rate loans.
  - It took effect for new loans from 1 Sep 2025 and for existing loans from 28 Feb 2026.
  - CBR is the fallback where KESONIA is impractical.
  - Banks must publish weighted average lending rates on their own sites and on the Total Cost of Credit (TCC) site.
  - Adoption is uneven: some banks stayed on CBR.
  - [CBK press release](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/uploads/press_releases/2044081907_Press%20Release%20-%20Issuance%20of%20a%20Revised%20Risk-Based%20Credit%20Pricing%20Model.pdf); [CBK KESONIA page](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/kesonia/); [CDH June 2026](https://www.cliffedekkerhofmeyr.com/en/news/publications/2026/Kenya/Banking-Finance-Projects/banking-finance-and-projects-alert-22-june-Kenya-2026-Banking-Law-Reforms-KESONIA-risk-based-pricing-and-the-mandatory-loan-transition-explained); [Kenyan Wall Street](https://kenyanwallstreet.com/co-op-moves-to-kesonia-as-equity-dtb-retain-cbr-under-cbk-credit-pricing-reform)

### Inferences
- What CredaFlow needs for Kenya:
  - Product and pricing versioning with a "CBK approval reference and date" gate before a product version can go live.
  - A 30-day notice-and-acceptance workflow for any term change.
  - An NPL-triggered **recovery cap engine**: freeze interest accrual at principal-at-NPL, and reset the cap on re-default.
  - A configurable payment-allocation waterfall, because Kenya's order (principal, then fees, then interest) is the reverse of common SA practice.
  - A KES-only currency lock.
- SA contrast: the NCA has hard caps (reg 42 maximum rates, initiation and service fee caps) and s.103(5) in duplum on all credit agreements. Kenya has no rate caps, but its recovery cap is arguably stricter, because only interest up to the principal-at-NPL is recoverable. CredaFlow's SA in duplum logic may be reusable but needs a different trigger (the NPL definition) and a different cap base.
- For bank tenants only, a KESONIA + K variable-rate model needs a reference-rate feed similar to the existing SARB ref-rate feed.

### Gaps
- Exact NDTCP 2026 regulation numbers for the cap, the allocation order and the FX ban.
- Whether NDTCP 2026 caps early-repayment charges (the draft had reg 49(4)) and what it says about repossession (draft reg 41). — [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/) lists these as draft provisions only.

## 3. Mandatory disclosures and contract terms

### Takeaway
Before granting a loan, the lender must give the borrower:
- a summarised **key information document**;
- full terms including **total cost of credit and APR**;
- a statement that it is CBK-regulated;
- receipts and statements on request.

No statutory cooling-off period for loans was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Reg 25 (DCP 2022):**
  - (a) Maintain a key information document summarising the product's benefits, risks and terms.
  - (b) Information must be fair, clear and transparent.
  - (d) Disclose CBK regulation on all channels and advertisements.
  - (e) Disclose the lender's identity on all correspondence.
  - [DCP Regs 2022](https://centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/L-.N.-No.-46-Central-Bank-of-Kenya-Digital-Credit-Providers-Regulations-2022.pdf)
- **Reg 27 (DCP 2022):** terms and conditions must be given before the loan is granted and must contain:
  - loan amount;
  - charges and when they apply;
  - interest rate and whether it is on a reducing balance;
  - all other charges;
  - due dates and how amounts are calculated;
  - total cost of credit (principal, interest, fees, charges, other liabilities);
  - **annual percentage rate**;
  - complaint channels.
  They must be in clear, simple language, with a copy on request. — [same](https://centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/L-.N.-No.-46-Central-Bank-of-Kenya-Digital-Credit-Providers-Regulations-2022.pdf)
- **Reg 21:** a receipt for every transaction, and a full statement on request. **Reg 28:** no false or misleading advertising, including on rates and charges. **Reg 26(2):** a marketing opt-out. **Reg 18:** an affordability assessment before credit is advanced. — [same](https://centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/L-.N.-No.-46-Central-Bank-of-Kenya-Digital-Credit-Providers-Regulations-2022.pdf)
- **NDTCP 2026:**
  - Agreements must clearly disclose the cost of credit and charges, and lending must be assessed against repayment capacity.
  - Providers must disclose the mobile-money account used for disbursement and repayment, any app platforms, and their bank details.
  - App-based providers must offer an opt-out, including from marketing after full repayment.
  - Restructuring is allowed under the credit policy.
  - [MMS](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/); [TechTrendsKE](https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/10/05/cbk-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations/)
- **AI decisions (reg 60, NDTCP 2026):** providers must explain automated decisions, tell customers when they are dealing with AI, keep human oversight, and address bias and transparency. — [TechTrendsKE](https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/10/05/cbk-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations/); [MMS](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/)
- **Financial Consumer Protection Framework:** the Joint Financial Sector Regulators issued a draft in March 2026. It is non-binding. — [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/)

### Inferences
- CredaFlow's NCA pre-agreement quote and credit agreement map well onto a Kenyan "key information document + T&Cs" pair. The Kenyan content list is shorter and is not prescribed wording, unlike the NCA's prescribed Form 20 and reg 30 content.
- The NCA's fixed document templates (s.129 notice, Form 20) have no direct Kenyan equivalent. Templates would be lender-owned rather than statutory, which fits CredaFlow's existing lender-owned versioned templates.
- Reg 60 on AI explainability is directly relevant to CredaFlow's decision engine and statement AI. It needs a reason-code explanation shown to the borrower and a human-review path.
- SA contrast: the NCA gives a 5-business-day cooling-off for some agreements (s.121, applying to lease and instalment-type agreements signed away from the provider's premises). No Kenyan equivalent was found for loans.

### Gaps
- Consumer Protection Act 2012 (Cap 501): no source found on whether its remote or direct agreement cancellation rights apply to credit agreements. Not researched to a conclusion.
- Whether NDTCP 2026 prescribes a standard KFS format or a TCC/APR calculation method. Not confirmed.

## 4. Collections and debt-recovery conduct

### Takeaway
Kenya bans debt shaming outright:
- no contacting phone-book contacts;
- no posting debtor data;
- no threats or obscene language.

Guarantors may only be pursued after borrower recovery is exhausted and after 14 days' default notice. Secured movable-asset (logbook) enforcement needs a default notice with a cure period under the MPSRA. No statutory calling-hours rule was found. There is no single s.129-style pre-litigation notice. The closest equivalents are the CRB pre-listing notice, the guarantor notice and the MPSRA default notice.

### Cited Findings
- **Reg 20 (DCP 2022):** the lender, its staff and its agents must not, against a customer or any other person:
  - (a) threaten harm to person, reputation or property;
  - (b) send obscene or profane language to the customer or their references or contacts to shame them;
  - (c) access the customer's phone book or contacts to message them about non-payment;
  - (d) post personal information online to shame;
  - (e) make unauthorised or unsolicited calls or messages to the customer's contacts;
  - (f) use any improper or unconscionable tactic;
  - (g) engage in any conduct that harasses, oppresses or abuses.
  [DCP Regs 2022](https://centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/L-.N.-No.-46-Central-Bank-of-Kenya-Digital-Credit-Providers-Regulations-2022.pdf)
- **Reg 26(1) (DCP 2022):** only data reasonably required for appraisal, approval, disbursement and collection may be collected. — [same](https://centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/L-.N.-No.-46-Central-Bank-of-Kenya-Digital-Credit-Providers-Regulations-2022.pdf)
- **NDTCP 2026:**
  - Abusive collection is restricted.
  - Non-judicial recovery must be exhausted against the borrower before pursuing a guarantor, with at least 14 days' default notice.
  - Material outsourcing controls replace the old agent-approval regime.
  - [MMS](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/)
- **Microfinance Act (as amended 2024):** bars non-deposit-taking lenders from harassing borrowers or contacting unrelated third parties. — [search synthesis citing The Star](https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2025-01-06-its-now-illegal-to-harass-borrowers-as-new-law-takes-effect)
- **ODPC enforcement on debt shaming:**
  - Whitepath: KES 5m (Apr 2023) and KES 250,000 (Mar 2025) for listing a guarantor without consent.
  - Mulla Pride: KES 2,975,000 (26 Sep 2023). The High Court dismissed its challenge.
  - Fingrow Capital: KES 200,000 (Dec 2025) for disclosing payslips, bank statements and ID to colleagues and contacts.
  - In Aug 2025 ODPC asked CBK to revoke two lenders' licences.
  - [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/); [HapaKenya](https://hapakenya.com/2025/12/04/fingrow-capital-slapped-with-ksh-200000-fine-by-odpc-for-exposing-borrowers-financial-data/); [techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/news/regulator-fines-kenyan-digital-lender/); [Kenyan Wall Street](https://kenyanwallstreet.com/court-dismisses-digital-lenders-suit-over-ksh-2-98mn-data-protection-fine/); [TechTrendsKE Aug 2025](https://techtrendske.co.ke/2025/08/25/rogue-digital-lenders-kenya-odpc-cbk-action/)
- **No CBK sanctions found:** no public CBK penalty, suspension or revocation against a licensed lender since 2022 was found. — [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/)
- **Movable Property Security Rights Act 2017:**
  - Sections 65–67 govern enforcement after default. Courts require a default notice that specifies the default, the remedy and a rectification period. A 24-hour cure notice was held not to be real notice.
  - One High Court view: repossessing without a court order requires the security to be registered under the MPSRA, in the e-Collateral Registry.
  - A Movable Property Security Rights (Amendment) Bill 2024 exists; its status is unknown.
  - [Kenya Law judgments e.g. Muhinji v Mogo Auto 2025](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2025/18928/eng@2025-12-19); [Mogo Auto v Ochola 2023](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2023/17527); [BRS e-Collateral Registry](https://brs.go.ke/?p=516); [The Star on 2024 Bill](https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/world/2024-09-18-new-bill-to-change-movable-assets-hire-purchase-laws)
- **Complaints volume:** CAK received 355 complaints against digital lenders in the year to June 2025, up from 67 the year before. A Mogo boda-boda petition reached the National Assembly on 18 Aug 2026. — [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/)

### Inferences
- CredaFlow's collections module needs to:
  - hard-block any outbound message to non-borrower contacts (references or guarantors) unless a lawful basis exists and, for guarantors, the 14-day notice and borrower-recovery-exhausted conditions are met;
  - have no contact-list ingestion feature at all;
  - support an MPSRA default-notice template with a configurable cure period, plus capture of e-Collateral Registry registration for logbook and asset loans, which slots into the asset-finance module.
- SA contrast: s.129/s.130 NCA is a prescribed pre-litigation notice with a 10-business-day wait. Kenya has no prescribed general equivalent. The Kenyan equivalents are:
  - the CRB pre-listing notice (30 days; 7 days minimum if contractually shortened);
  - the CRB one-month pre-NPL notice;
  - the guarantor 14-day notice;
  - the MPSRA default notice.

### Gaps
- Permitted collection calling hours and frequency limits: none found in the DCP Regs 2022. It is unknown whether NDTCP 2026 adds them.
- Exact MPSRA s.66 cure period in days: not confirmed.
- The NDTCP 2026 collections list: not read.

## 5. Credit reference bureau (CRB) obligations

### Takeaway
Lenders must share **both positive and negative** data with all licensed CRBs. They must give:
- **30 days' notice before negative listing**: a contract may shorten this, but under the DCP regs never below 7 days;
- one month's notice before a loan becomes non-performing;
- notice within 30 days of first listing;
- adverse action notices.

No negative listing is allowed at or below **KES 1,000**. Data is retained 5 years after settlement. Disputes must be resolved within 30 days.

### Cited Findings
- **Banking (CRB) Regulations 2020 (LN 55/2020, commenced 17 Apr 2020):**
  - **reg 18(2):** no negative information where the amount does not exceed KES 1,000.
  - **reg 26(1):** written or electronic notice at least 30 days before submitting negative information, or a shorter contractual period. Positive data needs no notice (26(2)).
  - **reg 26(4)–(8):** do not submit data the customer has disputed as inaccurate until it is resolved. Notify the bureaus within 5 days of a suspected inaccuracy and investigate within 14 days. Bureaus update within 2 working days.
  - **reg 26(9):** notify the customer within 30 days of furnishing data.
  - **reg 34:** NPL data is retained 5 years from final settlement, or 7 years from discharge from bankruptcy.
  - **reg 63(1)(a):** notify the customer at least one month before a loan becomes non-performing that it will be listed. For loans with repayment intervals under one month, the notice is two weeks.
  - **reg 63(1)(b):** notify within 30 days of first listing.
  - **reg 63(1)(c)–(2):** adverse action notice at the time of the decision, naming the bureau and the customer's rights to a free report and to dispute.
  - **reg 63(5):** amendment notice within 5 working days of learning of an inaccuracy.
  - **reg 63(6):** submit to all licensed bureaus or a central hub.
  - **reg 63(7)–(8):** a dedicated complaints unit, resolving within 30 days.
  - **reg 63(9):** penalty up to KES 1m.
  - [CRB Regulations 2020](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Credit-Reference-Bureau-Regulations-2020.pdf); [Kenya Law LN 55/2020](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2020/55/eng@2022-12-31)
- **DCP Regs 2022 reg 14:**
  - DCPs must share positive and negative data.
  - No negative listing at or below KES 1,000.
  - 30 days' pre-listing notice, or a shorter contractual period of **not less than 7 days**.
  - Post-listing notice within 30 days.
  - Data must be timely, complete and accurate.
  - [DCP Regs 2022](https://centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/L-.N.-No.-46-Central-Bank-of-Kenya-Digital-Credit-Providers-Regulations-2022.pdf)
- **Legal challenge:** the 2020 CRB Regulations were declared void by the High Court on 28 Aug 2023 (Justice Thande). The Court of Appeal reinstated them pending appeal (Civil Appeal E782 of 2023). No final decision was found. — [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/)
- **Licensed CRBs:** CIS ValiData was approved on 5 Aug 2025 and launched Sep 2025. — [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/)
- **Superseded moratorium:** CBK suspended negative listing for loans under KES 5m that fell into arrears between 1 Oct 2021 and 30 Sep 2022. This was temporary and is no longer in force. — [CBK press release](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/uploads/press_releases/566112348_Press%20Release%20-%20Suspension%20of%20The%20Listing%20of%20Negative%20Credit%20Information%20for%20Borrowers.pdf)

### Inferences
- CredaFlow needs a Kenya CRB module:
  - monthly full-file submission to all bureaus (Metropol, TransUnion Kenya, Creditinfo, CIS ValiData) or a central hub;
  - a notice scheduler covering pre-NPL (1 month, or 2 weeks for sub-monthly loans), pre-listing (30 days, or the contractual minimum of 7), post-listing (within 30 days) and adverse action notices;
  - a KES 1,000 floor check;
  - a dispute hold flag;
  - evidence retention of the notices sent, as reg 63(3) requires.
  This is more prescriptive than SA, where the NCA s.72 and reg 19 notices exist but the 20-business-day notice is a market norm rather than such a detailed multi-notice regime.

### Gaps
- Whether NDTCP 2026 restates CRB duties: not confirmed.
- The bureau reporting file format: the CBK-approved template was not found.

## 6. Data protection

### Takeaway
The Data Protection Act 2019 applies, enforced by the ODPC. Financial-services controllers must register with the ODPC. Fines reach KES 5m per contravention, and digital lenders are the ODPC's most frequent targets. **Localisation is limited to "strategic interest of the State" categories, and private lending is not among them.** Lenders may host offshore subject to cross-border transfer rules (ODPC 2026 guidance with standard clauses).

### Cited Findings
- **Registration:** DPA 2019 s.18 and the Registration Regulations 2021 (LN 265/2021, in effect 14 Jul 2022) require registration. ODPC lists provision of financial services as a mandatory registration category. Kenyans.co.ke names digital lenders, SACCOs and micro-lenders. Registration is tiered.
  - Conflict: a Dentons summary lists financial services differently. Verify against LN 265.
  - [Kenya Law LN 265/2021](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2021/265/eng@2022-01-14); [ODPC regs PDF](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-REGISTRATION-OF-DATA-CONTROLLERS-AND-DATA-PROCESSORS-REGULATIONS-2021.pdf); [Kenyans.co.ke](https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/77322-list-businesses-must-obtain-new-mandatory-registration); [Bowmans](https://bowmanslaw.com/insights/kenya-data-protection-getting-ready-for-registration/)
- **Localisation:** the Data Protection (General) Regulations 2021, reg 26 (with DPA s.50), require processing for the State's strategic interest to be done on a Kenyan server or with a serving copy in Kenya. The categories are civil registration, elections, public finances, protected critical infrastructure, basic education, and primary or secondary healthcare. ODPC's 2026 cross-border guidance confirms localisation is confined to these categories. — [ODPC General Regs PDF](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-GENERAL-REGULATIONS-2021-1.pdf); [Afriwise on 2026 guidance](https://www.afriwise.com/blog/data-without-borders-what-kenyas-new-cross-border-data-transfer-guidance-means-for-businesses); [Covington InsidePrivacy](https://www.insideprivacy.com/cross-border-transfers/kenya-issues-new-cross-border-data-transfer-guidance-familiar-concepts-but-important-local-differences/)
- **ODPC 2026 cross-border guidance:** issued in 2026 with its own standard contractual clauses. — [CaptainCompliance](https://captaincompliance.com/?p=13511)
- **Consent for data processing (NDTCP 2026 reg 58):** requires express, specific, separately obtained consent. — [MMS](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/)
- **Sector guidance:** ODPC published a "Guidance Note for Digital Credit Providers" (2024 upload). Its content could not be fetched. — [ODPC PDF link](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/ODPC-Guidance-Note-for-Digital-Credit-Providers.pdf)
- **Fines:** ODPC administrative fines go up to KES 5m. Lender fines are listed in section 4. ODPC reported a 75% fall in debt-shaming cases after the CBK rules; that report is about two years old. — [Kenyan Wall Street](https://kenyanwallstreet.com/debt-shaming-digital-lenders-drop-75-percent); [Mukamba Law](https://mukambalaw.com/digital-lenders/)

### Inferences
- CredaFlow's POPIA document-access log and DSAR module port conceptually. Two things are needed:
  - Kenyan statutory timelines for data subject requests: not confirmed, see Gaps.
  - Separate, granular consent capture: one for CRB sharing (CRB reg 22) and one for processing (NDTCP reg 58).
- Hosting on a South African or EU VPS is likely lawful under the cross-border transfer rules, using the ODPC SCCs, appropriate safeguards and a DPIA. This needs a Kenyan counsel check.
- SA contrast: POPIA has no localisation rule either, but Kenya adds compulsory controller/processor registration, which has no POPIA equivalent beyond the Information Officer registration. CredaFlow as a processor may itself need ODPC registration if it serves Kenyan lenders.

### Gaps
- DSAR response deadline under the General Regulations: not verified.
- Breach notification timeline: DPA s.43 is believed to be 72 hours, but this was not verified in this research.
- Whether software processors hosting offshore must register: not confirmed.

## 7. AML/KYC

### Takeaway
The governing law is the Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act (POCAMLA), with reporting to the Financial Reporting Centre (FRC), amended in 2023. Lenders must evidence the source of investment funds (DCP reg 30). Whether NDTCPs are designated "reporting institutions" could only be confirmed from a secondary source.

### Cited Findings
- **DCP reg 30:** a DCP must provide CBK with evidence of the sources of funds invested and show they are not proceeds of crime. Part VIII covers AML/CFT. — [DCP Regs 2022](https://centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/L-.N.-No.-46-Central-Bank-of-Kenya-Digital-Credit-Providers-Regulations-2022.pdf)
- **Reporting-institution status:** a compliance vendor states that every CBK-licensed DCP is a reporting institution under POCAMLA. This is secondary and unverified. — [FluxForce](https://www.fluxforce.ai/blog/aml-compliance-kenya-pocamla-cbk-and-frc)
- **2023 amendment:** POCAMLA was amended by Act No. 10 of 2023 and consolidated in a 2023 revised edition. The Regulations carry the operational detail: risk assessment, MLRO, and the cash-reporting deadline. — [FluxForce](https://www.fluxforce.ai/blog/aml-compliance-kenya-pocamla-cbk-and-frc)
- **SACCOs:** the Sacco Societies Act consolidation includes a 2025 AML amendment. — [Kenya Law](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2008/14/eng@2025-06-20)

### Inferences
- The KYC flow needs Kenyan ID and KRA PIN verification (IPRS lookup) in place of SA ID/Home Affairs and SmileIdentity's SA coverage, plus FRC STR/CTR reporting if the lender is a reporting institution.

### Gaps
- FRC reporting thresholds (cash transaction report amount), STR timelines, and the CBK prudential KYC guideline numbers: not researched to primary source.
- Whether NDTCP 2026 imposes AML duties directly.

## 8. Tax

### Takeaway
- **Excise duty of 20%** applies to fees charged by digital lenders (Finance Act 2025, from 1 Jul 2025). Interest is excluded.
- **20%** applies to other fees of financial institutions.
- Money-transfer fees are at 15% or 20%; the sources conflict.
- Financial services are VAT-exempt.

### Cited Findings
- **Digital lender excise:** the Finance Act 2025 (signed 27 Jun 2025, excise changes from 1 Jul 2025) imposes excise on "fees charged by digital lenders" at 20%.
  - The definition excludes interest, pre- and post-loan interest, and returns or profit share.
  - "Digital lender" covers anyone extending credit electronically, excluding banks, SACCOs and MFIs licensed under their own Acts.
  - Conflict: EY's Bill commentary and some practitioners said "fees and interest". The enacted text covers fees only.
  - [Kenya Law Excise Duty Act @2025-07-01](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2015/23/eng@2025-07-01); [EY Act alert](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2025-1498-kenya-enacts-finance-act-2025); [EY Bill alert](https://globaltaxnews.ey.com/news/2025-1057-kenya-introduces-finance-bill-2025); [Masibo Law](https://masibolaw.co.ke/2025/07/22/kenya-finance-act-2025-implications-for-startups-and-fintech-companies-in-kenya/)
- **Other financial-institution fees:** fees charged by financial institutions in connection with licensed activities attract 20% excise. Interest, profit share, insurance premiums and premium-based commissions are excluded. — [PwC Kenya Financial Focus 2023](https://www.pwc.com/ke/en/blog/excise-duty.html)
- **Money-transfer fees:** reduced from 20% to 15% by the Finance Act 2023, effective 1 Jul 2023. One later Business Daily report says banks reverted to 20% after the Court of Appeal nullified the Finance Act 2023. The current status is unclear. — [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/ke/en/blog/excise-duty.html); [ICTD factsheet](https://ictd.ac/wp-content/uploads/Kenya_DFS_Factsheet_0224.pdf); [Business Daily](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/tax-chaos-as-bank-fees-rise-state-defies-fuel-price-cut-4731104)
- **VAT:** no VAT applies to financial or mobile-money services; they are exempt. — [ICTD factsheet](https://ictd.ac/wp-content/uploads/Kenya_DFS_Factsheet_0224.pdf)

### Inferences
- The fee engine must split interest (no excise) from fees (20% excise). Excise is collected and remitted monthly to KRA. Statements and the KFS must show excise separately, whereas in SA the analogous line item would be VAT on fees.
- An NDTCP lending through an app versus a branch may differ in excise treatment. The "digital lender" definition matters, and a mixed-channel tenant needs per-product excise configuration.

### Gaps
- Withholding tax on interest paid to lenders, and on lenders' own financing: not researched.
- Finance Act 2026 changes: not found. Unknown whether any excise or digital-lender changes were made in June 2026.
- The Court of Appeal / Supreme Court outcome on the Finance Act 2023 and its effect on the 15% rate: unresolved.

## 9. Complaints handling

### Takeaway
Lenders must run a dedicated complaints channel, resolve complaints within **30 days**, keep a record of every complaint and its outcome, and disclose complaint channels in the T&Cs. CRB disputes have their own 30-day clock and dedicated unit. Escalation routes include CBK, ODPC, the Competition Authority (CAK, which handles consumer complaints) and the Small Claims Court.

### Cited Findings
- **DCP reg 22:** a complaints redress mechanism with a dedicated channel, communicated to customers. Complaints must be resolved promptly, or within 30 days where immediate resolution is not possible. All complaints and outcomes must be recorded, and breaches are sanctionable. — [DCP Regs 2022](https://centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/L-.N.-No.-46-Central-Bank-of-Kenya-Digital-Credit-Providers-Regulations-2022.pdf)
- **CRB reg 63(7)–(8):** a dedicated unit for credit-information disputes, resolving within 30 days. — [CRB Regs 2020](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Credit-Reference-Bureau-Regulations-2020.pdf)
- **NDTCP 2026:** requires complaints policies and mechanisms. No timelines are reported in the summaries. — [MMS](https://mmsadvocates.co.ke/the-central-bank-of-kenya-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations-2026/); [TechTrendsKE](https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/10/05/cbk-non-deposit-taking-credit-providers-regulations/)
- **CAK as consumer-complaint forum:** CAK handles consumer complaints against lenders (355 in FY to June 2025) and fined Mogo in 2024. The Small Claims Court dismissed 139 digital-lender recovery claims in March 2025. — [ITEdgeNews](https://www.itedgenews.africa/kenya-licensed-digital-lenders-now-it-must-supervise-the-loan/)

### Inferences
- CredaFlow's complaints module maps directly. Kenya needs a 30-day SLA, mandatory outcome recording, and separate CRB-dispute and data-protection categories. There is no Kenyan equivalent of SA's National Credit Regulator / Credit Ombud escalation letter, so the escalation text would point to CBK, ODPC or CAK.

### Gaps
- Whether NDTCP 2026 shortens the 30-day period or requires periodic complaints reporting to CBK: unconfirmed.
